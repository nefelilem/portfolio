§ 01 A bit longer.

I grew up in Cyprus, moved to Edinburgh for my undergraduate degree, and have lived in London since starting my masters at the Royal College of Art in 2024.

I trained as a product designer, first at the University of Edinburgh, then at the Royal College of Art, and along the way I kept ending up at the same place: the screen that sits next to the object. My final projects all became systems where a physical product and a companion app worked together, each doing the part the other couldn't.

That overlap is what I want to keep designing. I'm drawn to product teams that take detail seriously, the words an app uses, the weight of an object in your hand, what happens at 3am when something goes wrong. I think the best digital products feel like objects: they have presence, they're patient, and they don't shout.

Outside of design, I'm curious about quiet technology, anxious bodies, neurodiversity, and the way small everyday rituals shape who we are. A lot of what I make is, at heart, an attempt to design rituals.

I'm currently looking for a product / UX / industrial design role in London, where I can keep learning and contribute craft from day one. If you're hiring, I'd love to hear from you.
