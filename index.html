§ 02 About DESIGNER

Hello, I'm Nefeli. I trained in industrial design and gradually found my way into interfaces. Today, my work sits between the two. I'm drawn to briefs that take seriously the things people already live with, and I try to design effortlessness: objects and interfaces that belong where they sit, without asking too much of the person using them.

Currently looking for a product, UX or industrial design role, hardware, interfaces, or both.
